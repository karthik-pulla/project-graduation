What is Project Graduation?

Project Graduation is a parent- and community-run program that throws Hillsborough High's seniors one last party: a supervised, substance-free, all-night celebration right after commencement.

It's free for every senior, so the whole class can be there together. That takes about $100,000, which we raise all year through hackathons, fundraisers, sponsors, and a lot of volunteers.

Questions? We've got answers.

Who can go to grad night?

Every graduating Hillsborough High senior. It's a closed, supervised event, so guests from outside the class can't attend.

Is it really free for seniors?

Yes. Everything we raise during the year pays for the night, so no senior has to buy a ticket.

Where does the money go?

Venue, food, transportation, entertainment, security, and prizes. Our parent committee reviews the budget and shares it at info nights.

Can volunteering count toward service hours?

Most volunteer shifts count. Sign up through the events page and check in with the shift lead on the day so your hours get logged.

I registered for an event. What now?

Keep your ticket code handy. We'll email a reminder before the event with everything you need to know.
