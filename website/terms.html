Registrations and accounts

  • Give accurate information, and register only yourself or people you're allowed to sign up.
  • Keep your volunteer portal password private. Tell us right away if you think someone else has it.
  • Spots are first come, first served. Waitlisted people are contacted if a spot opens.

Volunteering

  • Volunteer accounts must be approved by an organizer before you can book shifts.
  • Students under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission and must follow school rules at every event.
  • Please cancel shifts at least two days ahead when you can.
  • Service hours are recorded by organizers based on attendance; we can't guarantee another program will accept them.
  • Grad night setup is limited to parents, guardians, staff, and alumni.

Code of conduct

Be kind and respectful. No harassment, discrimination, drugs, alcohol, or weapons at any event. Organizers may remove anyone who puts others at risk. Grad night is a supervised, substance-free event.
