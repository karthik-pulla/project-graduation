Effective [Month Day, Year]

Project Graduation is run by [Organization legal name], a [nonprofit / parent-teacher organization] that supports Hillsborough High School's graduating class. We collect as little as we can, use it only to run our events, and never sell it.

What we collect

  • Event registrations: name, email, your role (student, parent, and so on), grade if you're a student, and anything the event asks for, such as a team name, T-shirt size, or group size.
  • Volunteer accounts: name, email, phone, role, grade, a parent or guardian's email for students, an emergency contact, your interests and availability, T-shirt size, the shifts you book, attendance, and service hours. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash; nobody, including organizers, can see it.
  • Sponsor applications and event pitches: the contact details and answers you give us.
  • Donations and payments: handled by our payment provider ([Zeffy / Givebutter / Stripe]). We see your name, email, and the amount, but never your card or bank details.
  • Emails we send: a log of what was sent, when, and to how many people.

We don't use advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics that follow you around the web. See our cookie policy.

How we use it

  • To confirm registrations, run events, and manage waitlists.
  • To coordinate volunteers: approving accounts, scheduling shifts, taking attendance, and recording service hours.
  • To send meeting notices, reminders, and updates about events you signed up for.
  • To thank and follow up with sponsors and people who pitch events.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

Students and minors

This site is for the Hillsborough High School community. Students under 18 who register to volunteer must give a parent or guardian's email, and we let that parent or guardian know about the sign-up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you're under 13, please don't use our forms. Parents and guardians can ask to see, correct, or delete their child's information at any time by emailing hello@hhsprojectgrad.org.

Who we share it with

  • Organizers who need it to run an event or manage volunteers.
  • Service providers that help us run the site: website hosting ([host]), email delivery ([email provider]), and payments ([payment provider]). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Hillsborough High School staff, if you ask us to verify service hours.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk.

We never sell or rent your information.

Security and retention

The site uses HTTPS, the admin console and volunteer portal require passwords, and only organizers can see submissions. We keep registration and volunteer records until [12 months] after grad night, then delete them, except for records we must keep for donations, taxes, or service-hour verification.

Your choices

  • See, fix, or delete your information: email hello@hhsprojectgrad.org and we'll respond within [30 days].
  • Volunteers can update their phone, T-shirt size, interests, and availability in the volunteer portal.
  • Emails: messages about events you signed up for are part of running them. If you'd rather not get them, tell us and we'll remove you.
  • Californians and others with state privacy rights can exercise those rights the same way, by emailing us.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let registered users know. Questions? Email hello@hhsprojectgrad.org or write to [Organization legal name], [mailing address].
