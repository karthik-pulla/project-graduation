Effective [Month Day, Year]

We want this site to work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, or reduced motion.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. Here's what we've done so far:

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the stickers, highlights, and the rocket logo stop animating.
  • Text and buttons are built for strong color contrast, and pages work when zoomed to 200%.
  • Forms have labels, clear error messages, and don't time out.
  • Decorative stickers are hidden from screen readers.

Known limitations

  • Dragging stickers needs a mouse or touch, but it's purely decorative.
  • Some partner sites we link to (like payment pages) are run by others and may not meet the same standard.

Need help or an accommodation?

If something on the site doesn't work for you, or you need an accommodation at an event (seating, interpretation, dietary needs, and so on), email hello@hhsprojectgrad.org. We'll reply within [3 school days].
