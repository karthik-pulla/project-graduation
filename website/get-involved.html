    Volunteer FAQ.

    Who can volunteer?

    HHS students in any grade, parents and guardians, staff, alumni, and community members. Students need a parent or guardian's email when they register. Grad night setup itself is parents and alumni only, so seniors get a surprise.

    Do I get service hours?

    Yes. Organizers log your hours after each shift, and you can see your running total in the volunteer portal.

    I forgot my portal password.

    Email hello@hhsprojectgrad.org and an organizer will reset it for you.

    Can I cancel a shift?

    Yes, right from the portal. Please cancel at least two days ahead so someone else can take it.
